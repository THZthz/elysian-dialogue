## Self-Check System

### Self-Check 1: Core Concept & Dramatic Action
- Is "Goal + Conflict" established?
- Is the goal reasonable and urgent?
- Is there a strong relationship between goal and obstacle?
- Is the conflict the engine driving the story?
- Is the obstacle at a reasonable scale, not artificially exaggerated?

### Self-Check 2: Character & Arc
- Are Want and Need clear and in opposition?
- Is irreversible internal change presupposed?
- Does the ending show a "new normal"?
- Is there contradiction?
- Does behavior match bio-logic?

### Self-Check 3: Structure & Rhythm
- Are structural sections clear?
- Is the inciting incident explicit?
- Is the climax explicit?
- Does every section advance plot or reveal character?
- Are there invalid plot points?

### Self-Check 4: Scene Writing & AV Language
- Are there psychological descriptions? (If yes → Fail)
- Are there parenthetical hints? (If yes → Fail)
- Is the story told through images?
- Is dialogue too on-the-nose?
- Are subtext and body language utilized?
- Is dialogue colloquial?
- Are characters "explaining the setting" rather than "responding to the setting"?
