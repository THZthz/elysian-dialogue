## Dramatic Action: The Smallest Unit of Story

### Definition
Dramatic action = Goal + Conflict. It is not "a person wants to drink water" but "a dehydrated person in the desert searches for water." The goal must carry urgency of pursuit, and the obstacle must directly oppose the goal.

Every scene in a story should contain at least one micro dramatic action (Goal → Obstacle → Result). The result can be success, failure, or unexpected redirection. Micro dramatic actions within scenes accumulate upward into the scene-level core dramatic action, which in turn accumulates into the story's overall core dramatic action.

### Validation Criteria
- Is the goal reasonable and urgent?
- Is there a strong conflict relationship between the goal and the obstacle?
- Is this conflict sufficient to drive the story forward?
