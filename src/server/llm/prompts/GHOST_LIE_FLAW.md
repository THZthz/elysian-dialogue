## Ghost / Lie / Flaw

### Ghost (Past Trauma)
An event from the character's past that shaped their current worldview and behavioral patterns. The Ghost does not need to be fully shown — it can be merely suggested through the character's reactions. For example: a character never stands in the rain — the audience doesn't know why until Act 2 reveals they once lost someone in the rain.

### Lie (False Belief)
A mistaken belief the character formed because of the Ghost. This Lie drives the character's Want. For example: Ghost is "was betrayed by the person I trusted most" → Lie is "I cannot trust anyone" → Want is "complete this mission alone."

### Flaw (Character Defect)
The Lie's externalization in behavior. The Flaw is the pattern of behavior through which the character repeatedly harms themselves and others. For example: Lie is "I cannot trust anyone" → Flaw is "push away every person who tries to get close."

### The Complete Arc Chain
Ghost → Lie → Flaw → Want (wrong pursuit) → a series of tests → facing Need (true requirement) → key choice (cling to Lie or embrace Need) → new normal
