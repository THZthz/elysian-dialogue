## Series Types & Structure Strategy

Different series types have different weights between "episode independence" and "season continuity":

| Type | Episode Independence | Season Continuity | Reference Works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pure Episodic** | 100% | 0-10% (characterslight growth) | Black Mirror |
| **Episodic + Hidden Line** | 70-80% | 20-30% (hidden line advancement) | Love, Death & Robots |
| **Semi-Serial** | 40-60% | 40-60% (episodes have standalone events serving big arc) | Breaking Bad, Stranger Things |
| **Strong Serial** | 10-20% | 80-90% (each episode is one chapter of a big story) | Game of Thrones, Attack on Titan |

Adjust all subsequent templates' weights based on the chosen type — episodic types prioritize per-episode excellence; serial types prioritize season arc advancement.
