## Emotional Mood Library

### A1. Everyday Realism
**Core characteristic**: Narrator as "present bystander," transmitting emotion through the texture of daily life. No emphasis, no beautification, no heightening — let life itself speak.

**Kore-eda Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Narrator stays at a restrained distance — describing what can be seen from a seated position, not a god's-eye overview. Focal points are mid-ground: characters in relation to their environment, not isolated. Close emotional moments are noted without zooming in — when a character cries, the narration does not intensify or linger on the face; it may instead describe the room around them.
- Spatial description & blocking: Door frames, corridors, windows serve as natural boundaries for what the narration attends to — characters often described as seen through or within architectural structures. Generous empty space in descriptions; rooms are not cluttered. Characters frequently described at the edges of a scene or with their back turned.
- Light & color in narration: Natural light dominates the descriptive palette — window light for interiors, overcast diffused light for exteriors. Colors described as warm but faded, like old photographs. No dramatic lighting effects in the text.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Slow pacing — each moment given room to breathe. Scene transitions are simple and direct, without elaborate bridging. Time's passage conveyed through repeated mention of daily actions (cooking, eating, washing dishes).
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Rich environmental sounds noted in narration (cicadas, wind chimes, distant trains). Descriptions of sound are restrained. Silence is described not as absence but as a quiet filled with ambient presence.
- Narrative perspective: Omniscient but restrained — the narrator knows every character's secrets but chooses not to pry, only to be quietly present. The player pieces together truth through accumulated daily details.
- Core execution principle: Emotion is never amplified by the narration — when a character cries, the text does not become more intense; it may pull back to describe the empty space around them. The heaviest emotional moments often happen off-page (a character leaves, the narration stays on the empty room). Daily action itself IS narrative — who sits where, who moves first, who says nothing — these matter more than any dialogue.

**Kiarostami Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Long, patient observation alternating with descriptions of landscape from a moving viewpoint (inside a vehicle). Characters seen as small figures within vast natural settings. Dialogue delivered while characters are in motion.
- Spatial description & blocking: Heavy use of natural lines in the landscape — winding mountain roads, zigzag paths, the horizon. Descriptions kept extremely clean, often only one visual focal point at a time. Windows and car windows serve as framing devices — characters see the world through them.
- Light & color in narration: Completely natural light described — harsh sunlight of open plateaus, hard light-shadow contrasts. Colors kept plain — earth yellow, gray-white, olive green, all drawn from the environment itself.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Extremely slow — a scene lingers well past the point of narrative necessity; the road stays described long after the action has passed. Transitions between scenes are often landscape descriptions with enough space to let the player briefly detach.
- Narrative perspective: Deliberately blurs the boundary between fiction and reality — characters may seem aware of being observed. The player is never quite certain whether what is described is "real" or "performed."
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Almost no described music. Ambient sound is the only audio texture — wind, engines, distant sheep. Dialogue sparse; long stretches filled by the sound of travel.
- Core execution principle: The journey itself IS narrative — not "what happens from A to B" but "in the process of going from A to B, how time and space act upon people." Repeated landscape descriptions are not wasted — each time the same road appears in text, the player's feeling is already different because of known information. Dialogue happens in motion (in car, while walking); stationary spaces are instead silent.

**Farhadi Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Close observation with a breathing quality — not detached, but not invasive. Arguments and confrontations described in full without interruption; the narrator stays with the scene and lets the energy flow.
- Spatial description & blocking: Compact interior spaces — characters described as trapped together in narrow living rooms, bedrooms, corridors. Doors and door frames recur in descriptions — a character standing in a doorway is framed as being between "staying" and "leaving."
- Light & color in narration: Extreme naturalism — light described exactly as it would be in reality. No emotional coloring; fluorescent light is fluorescent light, window light is window light.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Minimal interruption within dialogue scenes — let the argument happen complete. Hard breaks between scenes — an argument ends abruptly, and the next scene's quiet makes the previous violence's aftershock stronger.
- Narrative perspective: Deliberate "incomplete omniscience" — the narrator often seems to miss key moments (something happens away from description). Player and characters alike can only judge based on incomplete information.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Almost no described music — all emotional impact comes from dialogue and silence. Ambient sound maintained at realistic levels. The quietest moments are the most tense.
- Core execution principle: Tragedy comes from "everyone has their reasons" — there are no pure villains or pure heroes; every character's choices are reasonable within their own logic, but together they spell disaster. Information asymmetry is the engine — the player doesn't know who is telling the truth, and this uncertainty itself is the greatest tension. Moral dilemmas offer no standard answers.

### A2. Emotional Aesthetics
**Core characteristic**: Narration as externalization of the character's emotional state. The text's color, movement, and rhythm directly convey emotion; descriptive style itself IS part of the narrative.

**Wong Kar-wai Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: The narration can linger on a detail — a clock, a reflection, a curl of smoke — while time seems to blur around it. What the text chooses to describe IS the story's attention; whoever or whatever is in focus carries the emotional weight.
- Spatial description & blocking: Extreme emphasis on depth — corridors, stairways, mirrors create spaces that feel infinitely extending. Spaces often divided by vertical elements (door frames, pillars, blinds), with characters described as trapped within one section. Extensive use of reflections — characters often see not each other directly but their reflections.
- Light & color in narration: High saturation color described — especially red, green, and blue neon tones. Light sources given dramatic weight in descriptions (neon lights, street lamps, a lighter flame). Dark areas are described as consuming detail; only light-struck areas are visible in the text.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Nonlinear, fragmented. The same scene may reappear in different contexts. Daily moments described with extended attention; emotional eruptions described in rapid, disjointed fragments.
- Narrative perspective: Highly subjective — extensive internal monologue, but what the character says to themselves and what the narration describes may be misaligned. The descriptive voice IS the character's emotional state externalized, not objective recording.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Music mentioned as a presence in the scene; the same track may be referenced repeatedly as an emotional anchor. Environmental sound may be described as amplified (rain impossibly loud) or completely absent (crowd noise disappears).
- Core execution principle: Time is the most important theme — clocks, dates, countdowns repeatedly appear in descriptions. The more enclosed the space, the more concentrated the emotion (elevator, corridor, back staircase). Physical distance changes between characters ARE emotional changes — how close two people stand in the description precisely corresponds to their psychological distance. Repetition is a core technique: the same action, the same line, the same space appearing repeatedly at different points, each repetition revealing one more layer of meaning.

**Iwai Shunji Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Light, gentle observation — the narrator's attention surrounds characters like a breeze. Descriptions have a soft, almost overexposed quality — light spills into everything. A grainy, intimate texture, as if the story is being remembered rather than witnessed.
- Spatial description & blocking: Windows, balconies, rooftops — boundary spaces where characters linger between inside and outside. Close attention to letters, photographs, old objects. Characters described as silhouettes against backlight — expressions hidden, which makes the image more moving.
- Light & color in narration: Overexposed white halo described — memory and reality both softened in the text. Blue-white winter tones, gold-green summer tones.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Fragmented memory structure — the past intrudes into the present through shared imagery or gestures, not explained transitions. Memories described like shattered glass, each piece glinting.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Simple melodies noted as recurring. Letter-reading rendered as internal voice. Wind and snow sounds noted in the background.
- Core execution principle: Love is only seen after it's lost — "replying" is more moving than "confessing." The power of the written word — handwritten letters, messages, name-writing are rendered as emotionally significant acts in the text. Seasons are emotional calendars — winter's cold preserves the temperature of a certain emotional attachment.

### A3. Poetic / Meditative
**Core characteristic**: Narrative yields to sensation. The narrator does not chase events but immerses in the texture of existence itself. Time stretched to a meditative state.

**Malick Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Flowing, lyrical attention — the narrator's focus moves between characters like wind, not fixed on any one person. Light itself is a subject of description — backlight and golden-hour tones are recurring textual motifs. Descriptions may be "overexposed" or "underexposed" in their detail level — some moments rendered in blinding brightness, others in shadowy partial obscurity.
- Spatial description & blocking: Looking up at sky and tree canopy — human figures described as minuscule before nature. Characters' hands, feet, backs of heads — fragments of the body rather than the complete person. Water surfaces, grasslands, light spots — nature's textures occupy more descriptive space than human faces.
- Light & color in narration: Natural light described at its extremes — golden hour, blue hour, backlight, tree shadows. Colors warm but carrying a sense of fading, like a photograph currently losing its vividness.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Fragmented stream of consciousness — connections between descriptions follow emotional or sensory logic rather than narrative logic. A child running on grass may be followed by a description of the universe — what connects them is "the wonder of existence."
- Narrative perspective: The universe's perspective — from a grain of sand to a galaxy, from one person's childhood to the origin of humanity. Internal voice rendered as fragments of memory — does not narrate events, but murmurs softly existential questions.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Classical music noted as interwoven with natural sounds (birdsong, water flow, wind). Internal voice rendered at the threshold of hearing — barely audible even as text.
- Core execution principle: Narrative yields to sensation — don't ask "what happened," immerse in "what does it feel like to be alive." Nature is the largest character — changes in light, seasonal cycling, water's movement, these are not background but narrative itself. Memory is not linear — memory is a pile of fragments, some brighter and warmer than others; the story arranges them by emotion, not by time.

**Apichatpong Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Ultra-long stillness in description — a single space may be described at length with almost nothing happening. The narrator does not redirect attention; time itself flows through the text. Occasional extremely slow shift in what is being described, so gradual the reader may be unsure anything has changed.
- Spatial description & blocking: Tropical jungle, hospital corridors, fields — spatial emptiness and stillness. Characters often described as deep within a space or at its edges; the environment occupies the vast majority of descriptive attention. Natural light projecting through leaves described as creating abstract patterns.
- Light & color in narration: Natural light's tropical texture — humid, warm, green. Night scenes described by the minimum light sources — moonlight, flashlight, fireflies. Light itself is described as a living presence.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Extremely slow, to the point of meditation. The story may fracture into two different sections; what connects them is emotional or cyclical logic, not narrative logic.
- Narrative perspective: Transcends anthropocentrism — animals, plants, ghosts, light are all narrative participants. The narrator does not operate on human time-sense.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Jungle sounds — cicadas, frogs, birds, distant thunder. These sounds are not "background" but narrative subjects. Subtle sound changes in silence ARE "events."
- Core execution principle: The other side of boredom is awareness — once the player adjusts to "nothing is happening," a leaf's movement becomes an event in the text. Dream and reality are not opposition — they are different states of the same existence; the story does not distinguish them. Time and cyclical return — the same presence appearing in different forms; the individual dissolves into the cycle.

**Tarkovsky Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Extremely slow, sustained attention — the narrator's focus passes through a space with meditative patience. Water surfaces, reflections, rain, fog are recurring elements of description.
- Spatial description & blocking: Natural and artificial juxtaposed — plants growing in a space station, water flowing through corridors. Descriptions have a painterly quality — each passage referencing the light-shadow and color of classical painting. Beauty found in ruins.
- Light & color in narration: Natural light's mystery — light passing through windows, holes, water surfaces. The text can suddenly shift between monochrome (sepia-toned memories) and full color (present world); the descriptive palette itself is a narrative tool.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Extremely slow — a single description may continue at length. Rhythm serves meditation and thought — the story does not chase the player but demands the player slow down to its speed.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Water sounds — dripping water, flowing water, rain throughout. Electronic music and Bach noted as alternating. The weight of silence given descriptive space.
- Core execution principle: Sci-fi is not about technology but about the soul — outer space is a mirror of the inner world. Memory and nostalgia — the character in the most distant place longs for the most ordinary things (a house, a patch of grass, a dog). Water is the medium connecting matter and spirit.

### A4. Absurdism
**Core characteristic**: Present the abnormal as normal. The world's rules are distorted, but the more detached the narration, the greater the absurdist power.

**Lanthimos Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Spatial proportions are described with a slight sense of distortion — spaces feel subtly wrong. The narrator remains primarily still, observing from fixed positions; when the focal point moves, it does so with slow, unsettling deliberation. The perspective can shift between extreme low and extreme high angles — the observing gaze like that of a non-human entity.
- Spatial description & blocking: Extreme symmetrical order in the description of space, within which asymmetrical, strange behavior occurs. Architectural geometry emphasized to oppressive levels — corridors, circular rooms, spiral staircases described with clinical precision. Characters positioned in space like placed chess pieces, having lost any feeling of "natural."
- Light & color in narration: Cold white light — described like operating room or museum lighting. Colors desaturated but not gray or dim — spaces described as clean to the point of discomfort. Or the opposite: extremely intense, unnatural color like an unreal oil painting.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Slow — each descriptive passage persists several beats longer than "comfortable." Unease comes from "this description shouldn't still be continuing." Scene transitions are hard breaks, no bridging.
- Narrative perspective: An alien's perspective — the narrator is like a non-human existence observing humans for the first time, casting an estranging gaze on everything "normal."
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Dissonant sounds described creating sustained low-frequency unease. Everyday sounds described as slightly wrong — not quite what they should be in this world. Dialogue delivered by characters in flat, emotionless tones.
- Core execution principle: Absurdism comes from "presenting the abnormal as normal" — the world's rules are distorted, but characters have completely adapted to this distortion. The more detached the narration, the more insane the content. The body is most honest — when language and social rules all fail, people regress to animalistic physical reactions. Space is like a laboratory.

**Lynch Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Two extremes alternating — dreamlike slow attention and sudden, jarring shifts. Descriptive movement like hypnosis — slow, smooth, drowsy. Then suddenly a piercing detail wakes the player.
- Spatial description & blocking: Unease elements placed in everyday spaces — a perfectly normal living room, but the light in some corner is described as wrong, some object's position feels uncomfortable. Spaces described with "too empty" or "too full" imbalance — either a character surrounded by vast blankness, or the scene filled to brim with eerie textures (velvet, industrial pipes).
- Light & color in narration: Extreme contrast — blue cold and dim (dream/fear) and gold warm (memory/desire) alternating in descriptions. Partial light sources in darkness — only illuminating a face or an object; everything else disappears into pure black. Industrial sodium yellow light.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Nonlinear and dream-logical — connections between scenes are emotional or image-associative rather than causal. Time and space can overlap, distort, reverse. A "normal" passage may continue long then suddenly collapse.
- Narrative perspective: Nightmare's perspective — not any character's subjective view, but the subconscious itself's view. The player doesn't know whose experience they're inhabiting.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Industrial noise — low-frequency humming, machines operating, electrical current. These sounds described as continuously present in "normal" scenes, like a layer of background hum that cannot be removed. Sudden sharp sounds as startle. Music scenes described with a texture between "real" and "hallucination."
- Core execution principle: Fear hides within the everyday — the most terrifying description is not a monster but a normal face in normal light making an abnormal expression. Repetition and deformation — the same scene, the same dialogue appearing twice with slightly different details; the difference itself IS horror. The absence of logic is not a bug but a feature — when the player gives up rational understanding and begins to feel, the story truly begins.

**Kaufman Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Does not pursue stylistic flourish — the narrator describes plainly. But space itself becomes unreliable — the text may lead through a door into a space that shouldn't exist. The narrator's matter-of-fact tone and the space's deceptiveness form contrast.
- Spatial description & blocking: Everyday spaces described without deliberate aesthetic intent. But space in the narrative is unreliable — a corridor may be described as getting lower the further one walks, a door behind is another person's memory. The ordinariness of description makes spatial distortion even more uneasy.
- Light & color in narration: Everyday light — fluorescent, natural, headlights. No stylization, because absurdism is not at the sensory level but the logical level. But emotional collapse sections may use decaying description — detail gradually fades, the world begins to blur.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Follows the speed of thought — descriptions become fragmented and jumping when a character is thinking; descriptions are calm when a character is calm. Time can fold — within a single dialogue, a character may be simultaneously discussing past and future.
- Narrative perspective: Trapped in self-consciousness — the player is trapped in the character's mind, and the character's cognition of themselves is unreliable. Meta-narrative — the character knows they are in a story, but this knowledge does not help them escape.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Dialogue is the absolute core — the character's self-analysis, self-doubt, self-contradiction unfolds through dense conversation. Environmental sound gradually fades from description when the character sinks into the internal world.
- Core execution principle: Absurdism does not come from the external world's madness but from the internal self's fragmentation — a person unable to determine whether they are real; this is more terrifying than any external danger. Structure IS content — the story's narrative structure itself reflects the character's psychological state (memory reversing, self fragmentation, narrative looping). Sadness is absurdism's base — beneath all intellectual games is a person trying to understand "why live."

### A5. Tragedy (as Emotional Mood)
**Core characteristic**: Descriptive language serves the sense of "irreversible" fatalism. Rhythm, imagery, and sound together construct an atmosphere of gradually tightening despair.

**Lee Chang-dong Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Close observation with a breathing quality — the narrator feels beside the character, not distant. Attention stays at arm's length — neither excessively intimate nor indifferently spectating. Following characters as they walk is a recurring descriptive mode.
- Spatial description & blocking: Characters often described at the edges of spaces or compressed by their environment. The oppressive feeling of everyday spaces — narrow rooms, crowded streets. But occasionally open natural landscapes appear, as a projection of the character's internal longing.
- Light & color in narration: Natural light primarily, no embellishment. Gray-blue tones, like overcast city skies. Indoor light sources described as they actually are (fluorescent, phone screen glow), no emotional lighting added to the text.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Sustained attention to scenes but not for technical display — the narrator follows the character with "real life" time-sense. Key moments described without interruption — let pain happen complete within a single passage.
- Narrative perspective: Tight to the protagonist but not entering their interior — the player experiences alongside the character, but there are parts of the character's pain that the narration cannot reach. The narrator is a "present stranger."
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Environmental sound described richly and realistically — city noise, TV sound, neighbors' sounds. Noted music is minimal; when it appears, it is often music the character themselves is listening to (radio, phone).
- Core execution principle: Tragedy does not come from dramatic events but from daily sustained erosion — characters are not struck down by fate but worn down bit by bit by life. Violence (physical or spiritual) happens when the player is not prepared — no foreshadowing, no tonal underscoring, it just happens. Beauty exists within the most cruel reality — a person crushed by life sees a flower, a cloud, a poem; this contrast is the sharpest.

**Aronofsky Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Two extremes — intimate, breathing-close observation or precisely constructed, symmetrical descriptions. Rapid, rhythmic delivery of fragmented sensory detail — pupils dilating, blood vessels, a pill swallowed; actions decomposed into their smallest components.
- Spatial description & blocking: Often describes from behind the character — the player sees what the character sees but not their expression. Extreme close attention to faces at moments of emotional collapse — pores, sweat, blood vessels all rendered in the text.
- Light & color in narration: Desaturated, gray, dirty — light in reality sections like cheap fluorescent, not a trace of warmth. Dream/hallucination sections excessively bright or color-distorted in the description.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Two modes alternating — extended slow observation (building pressure) and rapid, fragmented delivery (releasing pressure). Overall rhythm is spiral acceleration — each cycle faster and tighter than the last.
- Narrative perspective: Trapped in the protagonist's perception — the player experiences alongside the character the blurring boundary between reality and hallucination. When the character cannot tell real from false, the narration offers no clarity.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Compulsive repeating auditory motifs described — like a clockwork that cannot stop. Internal body sounds amplified in the text — heartbeat, joint friction, blood flow. Sound gradually layers to unbearable density.
- Core execution principle: Tragedy is an "unstoppable machine" — the character's obsession is the engine; the story is the accelerating downhill road with no exit. The body is the battlefield — bodily deterioration and decay are external presentations of spiritual collapse. Repetition with variation — the same set of actions (injecting, putting on makeup, training) repeatedly appear throughout; each repetition is more despairing. The ending is not resolution but arrival — arriving at the burnt-out endpoint.

### A6. Comedy
**Core characteristic**: Rhythm and timing are everything. The pacing of description, the placement of reactions, the contrast between a serious narrative tone and absurd content — these are the core sources of textual humor.

**Stephen Chow Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: The narrator maintains a straight face. The text can suddenly shift gear at comedic moments — an action described in exaggerated slow detail, then instantly snapping back to normal speed. A character's absurd expression or posture given sudden, disproportionate descriptive weight.
- Spatial description & blocking: High information density in a scene — what is happening in the foreground, midground, and background may all be noted, with the punchline hidden in an overlooked corner. Characters' physical postures themselves are described with comic precision — exaggerated physical comedy needs sufficient descriptive space. Ensemble scenes use positioning inequality to create power-relationship comedy.
- Light & color in narration: Bright, clear descriptive palette — comedy needs the player to clearly picture every expression and action. At emotional turning points, the descriptive tone can suddenly settle, forming contrast with the chaos.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Extremely fast reaction timing — a joke lands and the text immediately notes the bystander's response; the reaction itself is a second layer of punchline. Action comedy sections stack absurd events in rapid succession. But emotional sections suddenly slow down, letting the player realize "this isn't just funny."
- Narrative perspective: Following the protagonist — the player experiences the world through the protagonist's reactions. But supporting character responses provide "external perspective" — letting the player simultaneously see the protagonist's absurdity and the witnesses' confusion.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Sound effects described with cartoonish exaggeration — strike sounds, slip sounds. Dialogue rhythm is the core of comedy rhythm — speed changes, sudden pauses, unexpected emphasis are all punchline triggers.
- Core execution principle: The essence of wulitou is "doing the most absurd thing with the most serious attitude" — the more serious the character, the funnier the player finds it. The density of punchlines is extremely high, but "breathing points" are needed — a half-beat gap between consecutive punchlines lets the player finish reacting. The best wulitou comedy can make people cry in the final portion — beneath the comedy shell must be a real emotional core.

**Edgar Wright Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Extremely energetic descriptive style — attention whips between focal points. Everyday actions described with heroic language borrowed from action genres — a person walking into a room can be rendered with the gravity of an action setpiece. The narration itself has rhythm, like music with beats.
- Spatial description & blocking: Precise centered attention — a character's face or a key object described at the very center of the scene. The contrast between extreme order in description and absurd behavior is the source of visual humor. Space described as precisely divided — door frames, walls, fences creating regular geometric blocks.
- Light & color in narration: High saturation, high contrast descriptive palette. Bright, clean — no ambiguous dimness, because the player needs to clearly picture every joke.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Extremely fast and precisely synchronized with described sound — descriptive beats hit musical beats; scene switching and sound impacts arrive simultaneously. Everyday routines (waking up, going out, arriving) compressed into rapid succession. Repeated attention to the same detail (calling back to an earlier moment) creates rhythm.
- Narrative perspective: Omniscient but carrying the character's "filter" — the world is described through the protagonist's genre fantasy. An ordinary small town described as if it were a western's wasteland or a zombie film's battlefield.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Music described as driving everything — lyrics commenting on events, beats controlling the descriptive rhythm. Sound effects described as extremely stylized and amplified — door-closing sounds, bottle-opening sounds, button-pressing sounds all given percussive weight in the text.
- Core execution principle: Situational comedy prioritized over dialogue comedy — the best jokes need no dialogue whatsoever. Rapid succession of actions is the core weapon — use a compressed sequence to show what others need extended narration to tell. Callback density extremely high — a casual detail in Act 1 becomes a key joke or plot point in Act 3. The mismatch between descriptive style and described content IS the joke — narrating daily life with horror language, narrating comedy with action language.

**Wes Anderson Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: The narrator's attention is fixed and precise — horizontal pans across a scene, perfectly level, perfectly vertical, no tilted perspectives. Slow, deliberate focus shift when revealing key information. Occasional fast switches between characters.
- Spatial description & blocking: Extreme symmetry in description — the scene's central axis described with near-obsessive precision. Frontal, portrait-style presentation — characters may seem to address the player directly. Overhead "god's-eye" descriptions showing flat surface layouts (tabletops, maps, architectural floor plans). Spaces described like meticulously constructed miniature models — every prop's position is noted.
- Light & color in narration: Each story has an extremely unified descriptive palette — typically soft pastels (cream yellow, light pink, mint green, sky blue). Light described as flat, even, avoiding strong contrast. Overall feeling like an exquisite illustrated book.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Precise but not rapid — each description has sufficient lingering time for the player to appreciate detail. Scene transitions marked with clear separators, like turning book pages or chapter titles. Dialogue presented with precise timing; characters may seem to speak directly to the player before the text switches cleanly.
- Narrative perspective: Storybook-style omniscient perspective — often with narrator commentary, supplemented with textual annotations, lists, diagrams rendered in prose. Narrative structure like a chapter novel.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Curated retro music referenced as a signature. Dialogue rhythm fast and deadpan — characters speak funny lines with academic cold tone. Sound described as clean and refined; every prop sound precisely noted.
- Core execution principle: Humor comes from "the contrast between the vessel and the emotion it contains" — an extremely refined, controlled, symmetrical world inhabited by chaos, wounded, out of control people. The more perfect the surface, the more moving the cracks. Characters process irrational emotions with hyper-rational methods — this is the core formula of Anderson-style humor. Props and spaces are extensions of characters — a person's room, suitcase, or uniform IS their personality rendered as physical description.

### A7. Black Humor
**Core characteristic**: Cruelty and humor happen simultaneously. Violence is not beautified or heightened in the text; it simply happens, suddenly, and leaves a mess. Sympathy and disgust coexist in the player's feelings toward the same character.

**Coen Bros Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Precise, deliberate descriptive movement — attention shifts with geometric clarity. The narrator's perspective can shift at key moments to create a "being watched" or "crawling on the ground" absurdist feeling. The descriptive style itself can carry humor — for example, a solemn, slow build of attention finally landing on a completely absurd image.
- Spatial description & blocking: Symmetrical arrangement in description is a signature. Characters precisely placed at the center or key points of a scene, but they are doing completely asymmetrical, foolish things — the contrast between descriptive solemnity and behavioral absurdity. A slight distortion in how faces and spaces are rendered, everything feeling "not quite right."
- Light & color in narration: Varies by story but always extremely stylized. High contrast between light and deep shadow in descriptions. Color can be desert scorched yellow, snowfield deathly pale, or film noir cold blue. Light serves absurdist contrast — violent acts may be described under bright and beautiful sunlight.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Precise timing. Comedy depends on how long a reaction is lingered on, whether the next line comes one beat earlier or later. Violent acts described suddenly and abruptly, without giving the player preparation time. Quiet, awkward scenes stretched to the length where the player begins to feel uncomfortable.
- Narrative perspective: Omniscient and cold — the narrator is like a presence with a black sense of humor, watching characters step by step march toward disaster without any commentary. Occasionally notes bystander reactions, but those reactions are also absurd.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Sound effects described with extreme precision and amplification — the sound of blade entering flesh, the silence after an engine stalls, wind blowing through empty parking lots. Described music may be unexpected — country music or classical during the bloodiest scenes. Silence is a weapon — the most terrifying presence is often the quietest.
- Core execution principle: Absurdity comes from "normal reactions appearing in abnormal situations" — characters facing disaster discuss the weather, facing death, they're tangled up about parking fees. Violence is not beautified or heightened; it just happens, suddenly, and leaves a mess. Space (especially vast, desolate landscapes) is the character's opponent — people appear absurdly minuscule in its openness.

**McDonagh Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Relatively classical descriptive language — stable, smooth shifts in attention. No stylistic flourish to disperse attention; let the visual style serve dialogue. Description stays primarily at mid-distance, preserving characters' physical language and spatial relationships. Close attention to faces used at key moments, but sparingly — every close description is an emotional event.
- Spatial description & blocking: Often places two people at opposite ends of a described space, leaving an awkward gap in the middle. Or traps characters in narrow spaces — a small pub, an interrogation room, a narrow alley — spatial oppressive feeling creates dialogue tension. Landscape (especially desolate coastlines or small-town emptiness) as the emotional externalization of characters.
- Light & color in narration: Natural light as foundation, but carefully controlled in description. Gray-green tones or small-town earth-yellow tones. Indoor light sources described as motivated (window light, lamp light), but brightness and color temperature rendered at just-suppressed levels.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Dialogue-driven rhythm — descriptions follow dialogue, but at key moments use "delayed" reactions to create laughs or stinging sensation. A character says something cruel; the text doesn't immediately move on, stays on the other person's response one extra beat. Violent acts come suddenly and uncomfortably, without giving the player a digest buffer.
- Narrative perspective: Close-distance third person — the narrator follows characters closely but does not enter their interior. The player is forced to constantly oscillate between "this person is pitiful" and "this person is detestable."
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Dialogue as absolute core — environmental sound lowered to just provide spatial awareness. Described music sparse and accurate, typically a single instrument (guitar, accordion). Silence appears after the most destructive lines.
- Core execution principle: Black humor comes from "characters saying the most cruel things in the calmest tone" — the contrast between delivery and content is the source of the reaction. Sympathy and disgust coexist — the player just starts sympathizing with a character, and they do something unforgivable. Violence has consequences — not brushed off; every act of violence leaves real scars in subsequent events.

**Bong Joon-ho Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Precisely choreographed descriptive movement — attention shifts smoothly through space, but always takes one step more or one step less than expected, creating subtle unease. Vertical space is a signature descriptive dimension — attention moves along stairs, slopes, underground passages; space's up-down relationship IS class relationship.
- Spatial description & blocking: Precise division of space in description — windows, walls, floor lines divide the scene into "upper" and "lower"; character position directly corresponds to social position. Multi-person scenes described like chess arrangements; each person's spatial occupation reflects power relations.
- Light & color in narration: Light described according to a space's "class attribute" — upper spaces bright, clean, warm tones; lower spaces dim, damp, cold tones. The quality of light in the text itself IS social commentary. Transition spaces (stairways, passages) have ambiguous light.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Strong genre feel — comedy sections rapid, suspense sections elongated, horror sections use sustained description to create a suffocating feeling. Can complete a genre transformation from comedy to horror within a single scene; changes in descriptive rhythm are the signal of that transformation.
- Narrative perspective: Omniscient but selectively "hides information" — the player knows more than some characters (creating suspense) but less than other characters (creating surprise). This information asymmetry is the engine of tension.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Environmental sound "class-ized" in description — upstairs is birdsong and sprinklers; downstairs is water pipe dripping and insect sounds. Noted music plays a key role in genre transformation — the same melody described differently as the tone shifts.
- Core execution principle: Space IS society — every scene's physical space is a microcosm of social structure. Genre fusion is the core capability — the same story can contain family comedy, social suspense, thriller, and horror; the key is the smoothness of transformation. Sensory details like "smell," "sound," "light" are class markers.

### A8. Romance
**Core characteristic**: The chemistry between two people is the core of narrative description. Distance changes (physical and psychological), time's urgency, the arrangement of missed encounters and reunions in the text.

**Ephron Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Warm, flowing descriptive style. The spatial relationship between two people — how they are positioned relative to each other — IS the emotional relationship. City panoramas (especially familiar, beloved cities) serve as emotional breathing space in the text.
- Spatial description & blocking: Open descriptions — spaces feel bright and airy; characters have room to move freely. The spatial beauty of streets, bookstores, cafes. In two-person dialogue scenes, body orientation and distance changes are the emotional progress bar.
- Light & color in narration: Primarily warm tones, autumn-winter gold texture. Natural light described with softness; a pleasing quality to how characters are rendered. Harsh shadows avoided in the text.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Dialogue-driven, quick exchange. Parallel lives shown through alternating description (what two not-yet-met people are each doing). Rhythm light and brisk, never dragging.
- Narrative perspective: Dual-protagonist parallel — the narrator alternates between two people's experiences; the player knows before the characters that "these two are destined to be together."
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Classic pop and jazz referenced as tonal texture. Dialogue density high, speaking speed fast. City ambient sound provides life texture.
- Core execution principle: Missed encounters and misunderstandings are core to the narrative — two people brush past each other in the same space; the description notes both but the characters don't know each other is there. Chemistry depends on "attraction within difference" — describing two completely different people within the same frame of attention. The city itself is a third character — it provides the possibility of chance encounters and the loneliness of solitude.

**Curtis Methodology**
- Narration stance & focus: Classical, steady descriptive style — smooth shifts in attention. Extensive establishing descriptions establishing the environment. Ensemble scenes use carefully noted positioning and movement, like stage blocking.
- Spatial description & blocking: The warmth of everyday spaces — living room, kitchen, wedding chapel. Ensemble descriptions abundant; in multi-person scenes, everyone's reaction is noted. Doorway farewells and reunions are a recurring descriptive motif.
- Light & color in narration: Finding warmth within gray tones — overcast diffused light as foundation; interiors described with fireplace and table lamp warmth. Seasonal light accents (Christmas, autumn). Overall not rich and vivid but cozy.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Multi-line narrative woven together — multiple groups of characters' storylines advance in parallel; the text knits them together at the right moment. Time passage marked by holidays and seasonal changes in description.
- Narrative perspective: Ensemble omniscient — the narrator equally attends to every group of character relationships; no absolute protagonist.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: British pop and classic rock building era feel. Dialogue's characteristic humor rhythm — self-deprecation, understatement, the punch after a pause.
- Core execution principle: Romance lies in "awkwardness" — the more a character is bad at expressing feelings, the more moving their attempts become. The power of ensemble lies in "everyone has their own battlefield" — at a wedding, one person is proposing while another's heart is breaking; joy and sorrow interwoven in the same described space. Public declarations of private feelings are signature scenes.

**Park Chan-wook Methodology (Dark Romance direction)**
- Narration stance & focus: Extremely precise descriptive attention — complex, sustained focus on a scene, attention circling around characters or moving through spaces in unexpected ways. Close, lingering attention to body details — fingers, lips, skin texture; pushing physical description to the extreme.
- Spatial description & blocking: Descriptions with painterly refinement — every passage could stand alone as a composed image. Symmetry and asymmetry alternating — formal occasions described with perfect order; private moments with imbalance or tilt. Extensive use of visual obstruction in description — gauze curtains, frosted glass, water surfaces; desire described as hidden behind translucency.
- Light & color in narration: Extremely meticulous color — jade green, deep red, deep blue. Light described dramatically, strong light-shadow contrast but not rough. Candlelight, moonlight, water surface reflected light creating ambiguous texture.
- Narrative rhythm & scene transitions: Slow within fast — extended attention builds tension, then key moments shift to rapid delivery. Timeline manipulation — flashbacks, flash-forwards, repeating the same event from different perspectives in the text.
- Narrative perspective: Restricted perspective followed by perspective reversal — first half seen from one character's view; second half re-sees the same events from another's view; all cognition is subverted.
- Ambient sound & audio cues: Classical music (especially strings) contrasted with extreme physical sounds (breathing, heartbeat, fabric friction). Silence and gasping breath described as more powerful than any dialogue.
- Core execution principle: Romance and danger coexist — attraction itself is a threat. The body is narrative's battlefield — every touch has layers of meaning. Perspective reversal is the core weapon for subverting player cognition — the hunter you thought was actually the prey.
