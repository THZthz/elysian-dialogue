## Subplot Interweaving

A feature-length work typically has 2-3 story lines running simultaneously:

- **A Plot (Main Line)**: The protagonist's external journey — pursuing Want, facing obstacles.
- **B Plot (Subplot / B Story)**: Typically a relationship, carrying the thematic argument. B Plot's progression reflects the protagonist's internal change.
- **C Plot (Third Line, optional)**: Rhythm variation, or mirrors the theme from another angle.

### Interweaving Principles
- A and B lines converge at the Midpoint.
- B line's progression should mirror A line — when A line meets setbacks, B line offers comfort or counter-example.
- Don't let subplots become independent mini-stories — every line must ultimately serve the protagonist's arc.
