## Structure Selection Guide

Choose the structure methodology based on the story's subject matter and creative intent. Hybrid approaches are allowed — for example, use Story Circle as the skeleton and Save the Cat beats for refinement.

| Subject / Intent                                    | Recommended Method                    | Reason                                                                                        |
|-----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Commercial genre (action, thriller, comedy, horror) | **Save the Cat 15 Beats**             | Beats are precise to the minute, suitable for stories requiring strict rhythm control         |
| Growth / adventure / transformation                 | **Dan Harmon Story Circle**           | The 8-step circle naturally fits the "leave home → adventure → return" hero's journey variant |
| High character complexity, theme-first drama        | **McKee Story Principles**            | Centered on value shift and opposing unity, suitable for deep drama                           |
| Character-driven literary film                      | **Inner Beat Table** + style variants | Beats are internal state transitions rather than external events                              |
| Hybrid / uncertain                                  | Default to Story Circle as skeleton   | Story Circle is sufficiently universal and easy to understand                                 |
