## World-Building Three-Layer Model

### Surface Rules (revealed in the first portion)
What the audience and characters first encounter on the world's surface. "What is the basic way this world operates?"

### Operating Logic (revealed in the middle portion)
The deep mechanisms behind the surface rules. "Why is the world this way?" Typically revealed through the character's deeper exploration or the B Story character's perspective.

### Underlying Truth (revealed at the climax)
The ultimate truth of the entire world. "What is the world really?" This revelation should change the audience's understanding of all preceding events.

### Key Principle
Every layer's revelation must be naturally presented through the character's actions and discoveries — never through narration, subtitles, or characters sitting down to "explain."
