## Continuity Management System

Four tracking tables updated after each episode/scene:

### A. Character State Tracker
| Character | State at Episode End | Key Changes | Points that Must Continue Next Episode |
|---|---|---|---|
| [name] | [physical / emotional / relational state] | [changes this episode] | [points the next episode must respond to] |

### B. Hidden Line Progress Tracker
| Hidden Line Name | Current Progress | What This Episode Advanced | Next Advancement Plan (which episode) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [line 1] | [progress description] | [what this episode did] | E[X] |

### C. Seed / Callback Register
| Seed Content | Planted Episode | Scene | Planned Callback Episode | Callback Method | Completed? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [seed description] | E[X] | [scene #] | E[Y] | [how to pay off] | yes/no |

### D. Public Information Inventory
| Information Content | Released Episode | Release Method | Who Knows (character level) | Who Doesn't Know |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [info] | E[X] | [shown / hinted] | [character list] | [character list] |

### Usage
- After completing each episode/scene, immediately update all four tracking tables
- Before starting a new episode, first consult the tracking tables to confirm the current state
- If continuity conflicts are discovered, prioritize modifying new content rather than already-finalized old content
