## Arc Budget Allocation

An arc cannot happen arbitrarily across episodes — the "change allocation/quota" per episode must be planned.

### Arc Budget Table Template

| Episode | Arc Event | Change Direction        | Change Magnitude | Cumulative Progress |
|---------|-----------|-------------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| E1      | [event]   | Lie reinforced          | Faint            | 0%                  |
| ...     | ...       | ...                     | ...              | ...                 |
| E[N]    | [event]   | Key choice → new normal | Complete         | 100%                |

### Proportional Rules
- First 25% of episodes: cumulative progress does not exceed 15%
- At least 1 pseudo-change + regression (typically at the 40-60% position)
- 65-85% position is the acceleration zone — largest change magnitude
- Not every episode needs an arc event — in some episodes the character can not change at all
- Pure episodic series can weaken or even skip the arc budget, keeping only character state tracking
