## Subtext

Dialogue is an "iceberg" — what characters say is only the surface; the true meaning lies beneath the waterline. Avoid on-the-nose dialogue. Use action, silence, avoidance, and topic-shifting to express true intent.

**On-the-nose dialogue (wrong):**
> "I'm afraid of losing you."

**Subtext dialogue (right):**
> "Take that coat with you when you go. ... It's cold outside."
