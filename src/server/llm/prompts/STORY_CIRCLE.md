## Story Circle (Dan Harmon)

Suitable for: growth stories, adventure stories, transformation stories, stories where the character's internal and external journeys advance in parallel. Core logic: the character departs from the comfort zone, enters an unknown world, pays a price to obtain something, and returns transformed. The beauty of the circle lies in "returning to the starting point but being different."

### The Eight Steps

**1. YOU — Character in comfort zone**
Establish the character's normal state. The audience needs to understand "what is this person normally like?" The comfort zone is not necessarily happy — it can be a painful but familiar state.

**2. NEED — Character develops desire**
The character realizes (or is forced to confront) what they lack. This Need can be external (a task, a goal) or internal (a dissatisfaction, a longing).

**3. GO — Character enters unfamiliar circumstances**
Cross the threshold, leave the comfort zone. This is an irreversible decision — there is no going back.

**4. SEARCH — Character seeks, adapts, struggles**
Trial and error in the new environment. Encounters allies and obstacles. The character uses old methods to address new problems — sometimes it works, sometimes it doesn't. Corresponds to Save the Cat's Fun and Games section.

**5. FIND — Character gets what they wanted**
Corresponds to the Midpoint. The character achieves the surface goal. But this "getting" will bring unexpected costs.

**6. TAKE — Character pays the price for what they obtained**
After getting what they wanted, the costs begin to surface. This section is the bottom of the circle — the character is at the furthest point from the comfort zone. Internal and external pressure is at its maximum; the old self begins to disintegrate.

**7. RETURN — Character returns to familiar circumstances**
The character (physically or psychologically) returns to the starting point. But the character has changed — the old comfort zone no longer fits.

**8. CHANGE — Character has transformed**
Show the new normal after change. Forms a contrast with Step 1: same person, same place, different state.

### Unique Advantages
- Naturally possesses symmetry — the first four steps and last four steps mirror each other
- Step 3 (GO) and Step 7 (RETURN) are symmetric: departure and return
- Step 4 (SEARCH) and Step 6 (TAKE) are symmetric: seeking and paying the price
- This symmetry gives the story a sense of "completeness"
- More flexible than Save the Cat — not bound to precise minute marks
- Especially suitable for character journeys and transformation stories
