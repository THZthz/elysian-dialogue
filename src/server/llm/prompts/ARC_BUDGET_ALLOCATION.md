## Arc Budget Allocation

Arc cannot happen arbitrarily across episodes — the "change allocation/quota" per episode must be planned.

### Arc Budget Table Template
| Episode | Arc Event | Change Direction | Change Magnitude | Cumulative Progress |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | [event] | Lie reinforced |faint | 0% |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| E[N] | [event] | Key choice → new normal | Complete | 100% |

### Proportional Rules
- First 25% of episodes: cumulative progressdoes not exceed 15%
- At least 1 pseudo-change + regression (typically at 40-60% position)
- 65-85% position is the acceleration zone — largest change magnitude
- Not every episode needs an arc event — some episodes the character can not change at all
- Pure episodic can weaken or even skip arc budget, keeping only character state tracking
