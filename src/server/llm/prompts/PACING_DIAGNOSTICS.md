## Pacing Diagnostics Checklist

- Does the climax section's shot density form contrast with surrounding sections? (The climax should be the section with either the highest OR lowest shot density in the entire work — both approaches are valid, but it cannot be equal to surrounding sections.)
- Are there any 3+ consecutive minute segments at a single shot scale? (If so, needs a clear rhythmic intent.)
- Is there consistency in transition strategy across the work? (Same type of scene transitions use the same type of transition.)
- Have breathing scenes been preserved? (Don't let rhythm optimization compress all quiet moments away.)
- Does the longest single scene have sufficient internal rhythm variation? (A 3-minute long scene also needs tension-and-release variation internally.)
- **Restraint ≠ uniform speed** — even the most literary long-take style must have rhythmic variation across the entire work.
